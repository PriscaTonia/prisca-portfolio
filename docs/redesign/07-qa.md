**Notes on mobile performance:**
- Mobile uses Lighthouse's **simulated** slow-4G throttling.
- In the actual trace, the largest paint happens at first paint, so real users see the hero immediately.
- The remaining gap to 95 is the preloaded **DM Sans variable font (62 KB)**, which includes the optical-size axis the brief specified.
- Dropping optical sizing would likely push mobile above 95, at the cost of slightly looser letterforms at display sizes. **This is your call** (see "For our walkthrough").

## Bugs found and fixed during QA

1. **Lightbox kept the page scroll-locked after Escape.** Chrome doesn't always fire `close` after an Escape-triggered `cancel`. The clean-up now also runs on `cancel` and on the Close button. Commit `e9c0545`.
2. **Tap targets under 44px** on mobile. Affected:
   - back links ("← All projects", "All work");
   - "View all N projects →", "More ways to reach me →", "See the lab →";
   - card "Live site ↗" links;
   - project chips on About;
   - the wordmarks;
   - footer navigation.

   All raised to 44px. Commit `e9c0545`.
3. **Hero visual cost about 300ms of rendering on desktop** (live SVG noise filter). Replaced with a pre-rendered 11 KB grain tile. Commit `6d44e47`.
4. **An unused font style** (upright Instrument Serif) was being loaded. Removed. JetBrains Mono is no longer preloaded (labels only). Commit `6d44e47`.

## Known limitations

- **Theme toggle before hydration:** it renders as an empty pill of the correct size for a split second, by design, to avoid showing the wrong label.
- **Images and the live site's cache:** project images are served from Cloudinary through `next/image`. On the current live prisca.dev, Vercel's image cache may hold resized copies of the four images that were blurred, until they expire. The redesign doesn't reference those originals.
- **Article template:** `/writing/[slug]` is built and verified to type-check, but has no pages yet, because all posts are on dev.to.
